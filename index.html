Quantum Computing Basics

⚛ Qubit

A qubit is the basic unit of quantum information. It can represent a combination of 0 and 1.

🌌 Superposition

A quantum state can exist in a combination of multiple possible states until measured.

🔗 Entanglement

Two or more qubits can share connected quantum states, even when separated.

📡 Measurement

Measurement gives a classical result such as 0 or 1 from a quantum state.
